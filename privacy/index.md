# Snapjaw: Flytrap Game — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Snapjaw is a game about a hungry Venus flytrap. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Snapjaw does not collect, store, or send any personal data to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app contains no third-party SDKs.

## What stays on your device

The game saves a few things locally on your iPhone so it can remember your progress:

- your best scores, including daily challenge scores
- your fastest snap time
- your plant's growth and the color you chose
- your sound setting

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is a service run by Apple, and Apple handles that data under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive or store your Game Center information. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share** after a daily challenge, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text of your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

## Children

Snapjaw is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
