# Snapjaw: Flytrap Game — Support

Need help or found a bug? Email **[brama.ssh@gmail.com]** and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

## How to play

Your phone is a Venus flytrap.

- **On iPhone Duo:** the two halves of the inner screen are the jaws. Wait for a fly to land (it gets a glowing ring), then close the phone fast. While it's closed, the outer screen shows the plant chewing. Keep it shut until it's done, or the bug escapes.
- **On any other iPhone:** touch and hold the screen to snap the jaws shut. Keep holding while it chews.

## Tips

- **Bees sting.** Never snap on a bee. If a bee lands on the outside of the closed trap, wait for it to fly off before opening.
- **Golden flies** are fast and worth 5 points.
- **Snapping on nothing** costs energy. If the hunger bar runs out, the game ends.
- **Lightning snap (iPhone Duo):** fold really fast for bonus points. It also dazes nearby bugs so you can catch them next.
- **Combos:** catch something on every snap to raise your multiplier. Catch two bugs at once for double points.

## Questions

**No flies come to the trap on my iPhone Duo.**
Open the phone wider. Flies only come in when the trap is open past about 100°. The game shows "Open the phone wider" when it's too closed.

**When does the daily challenge change?**
Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone gets the same bugs in the same order that day, so you can compare scores with friends.

**How do I unlock new plant colors?**
Every point you score feeds your plant. As it grows, it unlocks Sunset, Royal, Ghost, Lava and Gold. Pick a color in the menu.

**I don't see leaderboards.**
Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then reopen Snapjaw. The Leaderboards button appears on the menu once you're signed in. Folding and touch players are ranked separately.

**How do I turn off sound?**
Tap **Sound on** in the menu to switch it off.

**How do I reset my progress?**
Delete and reinstall the app. All progress is stored only on your device.
